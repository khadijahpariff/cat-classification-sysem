# Ai-Powered Cat Breed Classification and Skin Health Diagnostic System

A web-based AI prototype combining cat breed classification, care guide retrieval, and preliminary skin condition screening.

## Problem statement
Cat owners and veterinary practitioners frequently face challenges in accurately identifying feline breeds and detecting early-stage skin health conditions due to a lack of accessible visual diagnostic tools. Non-expert owners often misinterpret overlapping dermatological symptoms or delay seeking professional care, allowing manageable skin infections to progress into severe complications. This delayed intervention increases overall veterinary treatment costs, heightens animal distress, and risks cross-contamination to other pets and humans. For instance, clinical veterinary studies show that over 40% of feline dermatological cases suffer from delayed treatment simply due to initial visual misassessment by pet owners.
